Brief About Us

The Society of Mechanical and Automotive Engineers (SMAE) challenges undergraduate and diploma engineers to research and develop innovative projects. We inspire students to imagine, as imagination is the foundation of breakthroughs in engineering.

Activities organized by SMAE give students the chance to form teams, work toward a common goal with team spirit, and showcase their creativity and technical skills against peer teams from colleges across the nation.

We bridge the gap between classroom theory and real engineering. Students learn to research, design, fabricate, and manage complex projects—gaining confidence to enter the automotive and mechanical engineering industries.

“Commitment unlocks the doors of imagination, provides vision, and gives us the right tools to turn engineering dreams into real-world innovations.”

— SMAE Leadership Council

Our Mission

  • Imbibe advanced technical skills among mobility practitioners and student engineers.
  • Promote scientific and technical development for the benefit of industry and mankind.
  • Create a vibrant forum for members to exchange innovative research and engineering ideas.
  • Provide guidance and networking from national and international industry delegates.

Our Vision

  • We strive to enrich technical skills and multi-disciplinary exposure of students by providing competitive platforms in mechanical, EV, and automotive domains.
  • To build a nation-wide network of skilled engineering leaders ready for future mobility challenges.

Core Pillars of SMAE

Innovation

Fostering creative design thinking and EV technology exploration.

Practical Skill

Bridging textbook concepts with real fabrication & workshop practice.

Team Synergy

Building leadership, project management, and collaborative spirit.

SMAE
