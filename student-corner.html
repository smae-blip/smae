About Student Chapter

Why Student Chapter?

Student Chapters encourage the promotion of applied engineering practice and research studies among students of mechanical and automotive engineering. The Student Chapter broadens the field of studies to apply the principles of science and mathematics in an effective way to solve real-world engineering problems.

It also encourages healthy competition and teamwork among all students and faculties in the society, creating a collaborative environment that bridges academic learning and industry practice.

How to Start

Guidelines to Open a Student Chapter

Your institute must meet the following criteria before forming an official SMAE Student Chapter.

Eligibility Requirements

  • The institute must be an AICTE recognised institute.
  • A minimum of 25 student members is required to open a Student Chapter.
  • There must be a designated Faculty Advisor for the Student Chapter.
  • A constitution must be formed for the Student Chapter, defining the roles and responsibilities of its office bearers.

Chapter Constitution

  • The constitution should clearly define the roles of the President, Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer.
  • It should outline the goals, meeting schedules, and activity plans of the chapter.
  • The Faculty Advisor must be acknowledged in the constitution as the academic supervisor.
  • The constitution should align with SMAE's broader mission of promoting engineering education and practice.
Leadership

Office Bearer Roles

Every Student Chapter must appoint office bearers who guide the chapter's activities and represent its members.

President

  • Communicate regularly with members, officers, and the Faculty Advisor.
  • Contribute to the common goal of increasing student projects and activities.

Vice President

  • Act as the liaison with the college administration, the College of Engineering, and the local Professional Section.
  • Contribute to the common goal of increasing student projects and activities.

Secretary

  • Contribute to the common goal of increasing student projects and activities.
  • Plan different events that will increase the skills of the students.

Treasurer

  • Contribute to the common goal of increasing student projects and activities.
  • Take care of all financial issues, if any.
