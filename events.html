Recent Technical Workshops Highlights

SMAE organized "Design and Fabrication of a Go-Kart" workshop hosted by Sri Indu College of Engineering and Technology, Hyderabad with 150+ participants nationwide. Another 2-day workshop hosted by Guru Nanak Institute of Technology covered engine transmission, braking, steering, vehicle safety, FMEA, cost reports, and business plans.

Technical Workshops

SMAE conducts intensive hands-on workshops covering CAD/CAM design, chassis analysis, brake geometry setup, and electric powertrain integration across colleges in India.

Key highlights include 2-day workshops at Sri Indu College of Engineering and Guru Nanak Institute of Technology with 200+ participants undergoing training in steering systems, FMEA failure mode analysis, safety protocols, and cost reports.

Seminars & Conferences

SMAE organizes national-level conferences to foster academic research, innovation, and career opportunities in Mechanical and Automobile Engineering.

Delegates from top aerospace and automotive firms join our seminars to share key insights on EV battery safety, vehicle dynamics, and sustainable mobility.

Training Sessions & Internships

SMAE offers summer and winter industrial training programs in association with automotive partners such as CARJOZ, providing students with direct industry exposure.

Awareness & Up-Skill Programs

SMAE conducts community outreach, student chapter installations, and up-skill programs for young engineering minds, introducing them to future green mobility concepts.
