About the Award

What is the PVNR Young Leader Award?

The PVNR Young Leader Award is a tribute to the vision of former Prime Minister of India, Mr. P V Narasimha Rao, and was initiated on his 100th birth anniversary. The award is aimed at recognizing and encouraging young professionals who are working towards community development through sustainability and technology.

The award is given to individuals between the ages of 17 and 40 who are making significant contributions to the fields of sustainability and technology. It recognizes the outstanding achievements of technologists, entrepreneurs, academicians, and other passionate individuals who are fulfilling the vision of Sustainable Development.

The award ceremony is held annually and has become one of the most prestigious events in the field of sustainability and technology. It not only recognizes the achievements of the recipients but also inspires others to work towards the betterment of society through sustainable development and technological advancements.

SMAE, the organization behind the PVNR Young Leader Award, is committed to promoting sustainable development and technological advancements for the betterment of society. Through this award, SMAE hopes to encourage and inspire young professionals to take up the challenge of creating a sustainable future for all.

How We Choose

The Selection Process

Award recipients are chosen through a rigorous selection process involving a panel of experts from the field of sustainability and technology.

A dedicated panel of experts from the fields of sustainability and technology evaluates each nominee against a set of defined criteria. The process is designed to identify individuals who have made genuine, measurable, and inspiring contributions to their fields and communities.

1

Impact of Work

The measurable difference made by the individual's work in their community, industry, or society at large — particularly in sustainability and technology.

2

Innovation

The originality and creative thinking behind the individual's work — new ideas, approaches, technologies, or business models that push boundaries.

3

Leadership Qualities

The ability to inspire, motivate, and lead others toward a common goal — demonstrated through their work, team, and community impact.

Our Honourees

Previous Awardees

Four inspiring young leaders have been recognized since the award's inaugural year in 2021.

RC
Mr. Raghuveer Chadalavada
Founder & CEO — Aurita Bikes
2021

Raghuveer Chadalavada's journey has been humble, but the learnings have been immense. A self-motivated, results-oriented entrepreneur, his love for technology and mobility led to the formation of Aurita Bikes in 2018 — with a vision to spearhead a revolution in the electric vehicle space and offer sustainable mobility solutions.

Over the years, exploring innovative ways to transition successfully has been his driving force. With hands-on experience in product development, technology, marketing, finance, people management, sales, customer support, and compliance, he has taken Aurita from 0 to 1 and 1 to 100.

In 2015, he began building real vehicles for Nitroshockwaves, his college's formula racing team, where he served as the lead vehicle designer. This experience marked the beginning of his journey in vehicle designing, manufacturing, testing, and competing.

During this course, he single-handedly managed a team of 25+ people. He and his team are dedicated to delivering utility vehicles with the lowest total cost of ownership and the highest level of utility.

Mr. Abhay Chakra Sadineni
Co-Founder & CEO — Natulatech Solutions Pvt Ltd
President — The Circular  |  Director IRC — DESC
2022

Director of International Relations and Communications at the Digital Education and Skills Council (DESC), Abhay led the Nationwide Health Surveillance Program and is a founding member of the Centre for Global Health Security. He co-founded a deep-tech startup specializing in bio-mimetic quality control solutions.

He introduced Lotuster — an omni-liquid quality control device — and is currently working on democratizing biomimetic fluid diodes in healthcare. His startup was selected as a top deep-tech venture at Venture Challenge 5.0 at SNU and received full-time incubation support.

As the Executive Director of the CareGood Foundation, he enhances students' well-being. He is President at "The Circular", a Hyderabad-based non-profit creating livelihood opportunities and engaging in sustainability initiatives.

Abhay graduated in Mechanical Engineering and received the Academic Excellence Award. He is passionate about advancing health equity and recognized for promoting sustainability.

TB
Dr. Tusharika Sinha Banerjee
Founder — Sweekriti Asset  |  Associate Professor — Pillai College of Engineering
2023

An accomplished educator and researcher with 16 years of teaching experience at graduate and postgraduate levels, Dr. Tusharika S. Banerjee has guided numerous projects and published 20 papers in prestigious journals including Elsevier, Springer, and Wiley.

Her work has been cited by institutions including NASA, the Astrophysics Data System (ADS), Harvard University, and is included in the Cornell University Library.

In recognition of her exceptional contributions, Dr. Banerjee was awarded the Bharat Shiksha Ratan in 2018 by the National Achievers Forum. In 2021, she received the Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Teacher's Excellence Award from AICTE.

She is also the founder of SWEEKRITI ASSET LLP, approved by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India. The company focuses on Research and Development, providing products and services that help people with disabilities find employment, offering training programs, and providing products that aid in water conservation.
