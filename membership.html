Student Membership Guidelines

The Member must be pursuing education in the fields of Mechanical and Automobile Engineering from an AICTE recognised organisation. This membership is valid up to Graduation. Our Executive Council has the right to refuse the application and to cancel the membership.

Guidelines

  • Must be pursuing Mechanical / Automobile Engineering from an AICTE recognised institution.
  • Membership valid until Graduation.
  • Executive Council reserves the right to refuse or cancel membership.

Benefits

  • Gain practical skills and hands-on experience beyond the classroom.
  • Publish technical articles in SMAE publications.
  • Participate in SMAE discussion forums.
  • Receive an official SMAE Membership Certificate.

Life Membership Guidelines

Member must hold a Degree and be pursuing a professional career in Mechanical or Automotive Engineering. Our Executive Council has the right to refuse the application and to cancel the membership. Life Membership offers expanded access to SMAE's resources and governance opportunities.

Guidelines

  • Must hold a recognised Degree in Mechanical / Automotive Engineering.
  • Must be pursuing a professional career in the related field.
  • Executive Council reserves the right to refuse or cancel membership.

Benefits

  • Participate in SMAE discussion forums.
  • Publish technical articles in SMAE publications.
  • 50% fee waiver on workshops and conferences.
  • Receive the monthly SMAE Newsletter.
  • Opportunity to serve on the SMAE Executive Committee.
